What is Trade Profit Fund?

A trade profit fund is a financial and accounting framework established by law to enable to the adoption of certain accounting and management practices common in the private sector. The fund operates on a self-financing basis and does not need to regularly seek funding from the legislature to finance its daily operations after its establishment.... the intention is that such an institutional change would provide the appropriate flexibility in resource management and nurture a new working culture to improve services in terms of both quality and cost-effectiveness.

What is TPF?

It's more like the surplus funds of a company which usually doesn't appear in any of the company’s balance sheets so basically, you can assume it is black money. Now they need to convert black into white, so they prefer to provide this amount as an unsecured loan to NGOs with some % deduction in return but they do all sorts of formalities in terms of documentation and the entire process is legally followed. In return, NGO gives them liquid cash after a few days company will close the loan case by showing partial payment received and bankruptcy of that NGO
